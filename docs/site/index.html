An independent waste calendar for Android

Know your next
ophaaldag.

See upcoming collections, check what goes in each bin, and set reminders. Ophaaldag uses the Cure Afvalbeheer waste service for supported addresses in Eindhoven, Geldrop-Mierlo and Valkenswaard.

Built around your collection day

  • A clear home screen and monthly calendar
  • Waste sorting information and nearby containers
  • Optional reminders and a home screen widget
  • Optional calendar sync, with new dates added when the service publishes them
  • English and Dutch, light and dark themes

Privacy by design

No advertising, analytics SDK, app account or developer-run server. Your address is sent to the waste service to find your collection schedule. Calendar access is optional.
