Ophaaldag helps you keep track of waste collections for supported addresses in Eindhoven, Geldrop-Mierlo and Valkenswaard.

• See your next pickup and browse a monthly calendar.
• Set optional collection reminders.
• Read waste sorting guidance and find nearby containers.
• Add a home screen widget.
• Sync pickup events to a calendar you choose, or export a one-time .ics file.
• Use English or Dutch, light or dark themes, and Android dynamic colours.

New calendar dates appear when the waste service publishes them. Background updates depend on Android scheduling and network access. An imported .ics file does not update automatically.

No ads, analytics SDK or app account. Your address is sent to api.mijnafvalwijzer.nl to retrieve your schedule. Calendar access and notifications are optional.
