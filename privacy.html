Privacy policy

Ophaaldag · Effective 19 September 2026

Ophaaldag is an independent Android app developed by Pere Gomila. It is not affiliated with Cure Afvalbeheer or AddComm. Contact: pegoku08@gmail.com.

Address and waste collection data

When you enter a postcode, Ophaaldag sends it to api.mijnafvalwijzer.nl to find street names. When you continue, it sends your postcode, house number, suffix and selected street to the same third-party service to retrieve your collection schedule. Requests use HTTPS. The service also receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address. Its operator controls its own processing and retention; Ophaaldag does not control that service.

Your selected address, preferences and the latest service response are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The response can include address coordinates, collection dates, waste information and public service messages. Coordinates returned for the address are used locally to sort nearby containers. Ophaaldag does not request device location access.

Background updates and notifications

The app periodically repeats the address lookup to update your schedule, including while the app is closed. Android may delay this work. Notifications are optional and are generated on your device for collection reminders and new service messages. Ophaaldag does not register a device token with a developer-run push service.

Optional calendar access

If you enable calendar sync, the app requests calendar permissions after showing an explanation. It reads calendar names and account names so you can choose a calendar. It writes, replaces and deletes its own pickup events and reminders in that calendar. It does not read the contents of unrelated events or send calendar contents to the waste service or developer.

Your calendar provider, such as Google, may sync the created events to your account and other devices under that provider’s policies. Turn calendar sync off in Ophaaldag, while permission is still granted, to remove its events. Removing the app alone does not remove events already saved in an external calendar.

Exporting an .ics file is optional. The file includes pickup dates and the resolved address in its calendar name. It is passed to the app or recipient you choose and is a one-time snapshot. Exported copies are controlled by you and the recipient.

External links

Opening maps passes the selected public container location to your chosen maps app. Other links open in your browser. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

No advertising or developer telemetry

The app contains no ads, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK, and has no app account. The developer does not operate a server that receives your address or calendar data. Google Play and Android may process installation, diagnostic and crash information under their own settings and policies.

Retention, backup and deletion

Address preferences and cached data remain on the device until replaced or removed. Changing the address replaces the local schedule. Clearing Ophaaldag’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes its local data. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer by the app’s backup rules. Copies exported or synchronized to other services must be removed there. Contact the waste service for requests concerning data it holds.

Children and changes

Ophaaldag is a general utility and is not designed specifically for children. If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
